Squishy Ride

Bounce, splat and paint

A squishy little critter bounces up an endless tower of xylophone bars. Every bar plays a note and every landing splats paint. You steer, make critters of your own, and paint the wall at home.

Support

Found a bug, lost your progress, or have a question? Write to salsasharkapps@icloud.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you're using.

Tips

  • Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer.
  • Drop a critter into the round start hole to begin a run.
  • Kids mode (the gear on the play page) takes out the saws, widens the bars and adds three rescue bubbles per run.
  • To delete a critter you made, drop it into the saw in the floor of the make page.

Your progress

Your progress, critters and paintings are saved on your iPhone. They are kept when the game updates, and they come back if you restore your iPhone from an iCloud or device backup. They aren't synced between devices. Deleting the game deletes them.
